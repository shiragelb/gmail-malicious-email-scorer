What this test is for

Three fixed emails check that the scorer can tell a real message from classic phishing and from quieter targeted phishing. Each case has a locked band. If the score lands in that band, the case passes.

  • A proves a clean, authenticated email stays Safe (0–15) — it must not cry wolf.
  • B proves failed auth plus a reserved malicious URL is called Malicious (85–100).
  • C proves a polite lookalike login still raises Suspicious (60–80) even when SPF/DKIM pass.
